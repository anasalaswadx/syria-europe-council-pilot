# Syria Europe Council — Pilot

## [Open the live website →](https://anasalaswadx.github.io/syria-europe-council-pilot/)

An Arabic, responsive pilot website for **Syria Europe Council** — an independent initiative coordinating Syrian expertise, initiatives and partnerships across Europe, starting from Belgium.

The pilot covers:

- Networks, trust and coordination across Europe
- Investment connections and responsible economic cooperation
- Knowledge exchange, governance and recovery expertise
- Integration, inclusion and social cohesion in Europe
- Practical examples, frequently asked questions and a front-end contact form

The contact form prepares a message in the visitor's email application; it does not transmit or store data on a server.

---

**Pilot status:** The activities described are illustrative and intended to gather feedback from the founding team and potential partners.
